## Pick

Candidate 1. It speaks directly to the brief's own image (the meeting that
should have been an email) and flips it: the email is no escape, because
the email itself would need a meeting. That recursive loop is the punchline —
a specific, recognisable office absurdity rather than a generic complaint.
Candidates 2 and 3 are situational but their punchlines land softer (a list,
then a seminar). Candidate 4 rambles slightly and its payoff is muddled.
Candidate 5 leans on the well-worn "per my last email" meme. Candidate 6 is
the same loop as the pick but stated flatly, with no surprise turn.
Candidate 7 is a neat observation but reads as a quip, not a joke with a
punchline.
